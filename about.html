Patrumin Investors

An independent, registered investment adviser.

Patrumin Investors LLC (“the Firm” or “Patrumin”) is an independent investment adviser registered under the Investment Advisers Act of 1940. The Firm was founded in September 2012 and is owned by Samuel Dedio.

Patrumin Investors operates as an independent investment management company specializing in U.S. equities, and offers equity-focused portfolio strategies, including the Patrumin U.S. Dividends Plus+ Equity Strategy, Patrumin U.S. Smallcap Equity Strategy, Patrumin U.S. Allcap Equity Strategy, and Patrumin U.S. SMICROCAP Equity Strategy.

The Firm also manages strategies that balance equities and fixed income, including Custom Balanced Equity-Income and Custom Balanced strategies designed around each client’s income objectives, risk tolerance, and investment time horizon. The Firm manages discretionary accounts for institutional and retail clients.

Patrumin’s investment management team brings more than 30 years of experience identifying companies with durable competitive advantages and superior free cash flow characteristics. The research process combines proprietary company analysis, direct management engagement, industry conferences, and rigorous financial modeling.
