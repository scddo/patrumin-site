# RIA Compliance & Positioning Guidelines

## SEC / Regulatory Boundaries
- **No Performance Guarantees:** Never use words like "guaranteed," "risk-free," "best returns," or "top-performing."
- **Fiduciary Clarity:** Expressly state that the firm acts as a fiduciary held to legal standards of loyalty and care.
- **Required Footer Links:**
  - Form ADV Part 2A & 2B Link
  - Form CRS (Client Relationship Summary) Link
  - Standard SEC Disclaimer: "Investment advisory services offered through [Firm Name], a Registered Investment Advisor. Past performance is no guarantee of future results."

## Visual & Design Standards
- **Color Palette:** Trust-focused tones (Deep Blue, Emerald Green, Rich Charcoal, Warm Ochre/Gold accents). Avoid bright neon or overly aggressive red tones.
- **Typography:** Serif or clean Sans-Serif headers (e.g., Playfair Display, Merriweather, Inter, Plus Jakarta Sans) paired with highly legible body text.
- **Imagery:** Authentic human elements, peaceful retirement scenarios, clear financial roadmap imagery over generic abstract charts.
