# Conversion Copy Frameworks for Wealth Management

## The 5-Second Rule
A visitor landing on the homepage must understand three things within 5 seconds:
1. Who this is for (e.g., business owners, retirees, tech executives).
2. What primary problem is solved (e.g., tax minimization, retirement income security).
3. What step to take next (e.g., schedule a call).

## High-Converting Copy Formula
- **Instead of:** "We offer comprehensive wealth management services."
- **Use:** "Personalized Wealth Management Built to Protect and Grow Your Family's Legacy."

- **Instead of:** "Contact us today."
- **Use:** "Schedule Your Complimentary Wealth & Tax Review."
